The model's rules

A projection is not a bet. We distinguish independent score estimates, historical backtesting and sportsbook lines.

Truth over confidence. No predictions from fake sample data. No published win rate without real, chronological, held-out games. Missing market lines are labeled, not invented.

How this works

Import per-game player statistics and use the estimate button to populate a recency-weighted expectation and observed game-to-game variability. Manual adjustments remain available.

ATTD · P(≥1 TD) = 1 − exp(−λ); includes rushing/receiving TD, not passing TD.
Receptions · Discrete Poisson probability above/below a half-catch line.
Yards · Normal probability using projected yards and an assumed standard deviation.
Odds · Convert American odds to break-even probability and expected net return per unit stake.
Two-sided odds · Estimate no-vig market probability if both over and under prices are entered.
Building the real algorithm: This is a historical box-score baseline, not a fitted or calibrated betting model. A stronger version still needs actual snaps, routes, target share, red-zone opportunities, injuries, defensive matchups and timestamped sportsbook prices. ATTD goals here cannot fully account for teammate usage or injury changes.

How to read results

A 55% cover-probability estimate is a model output, not evidence of a 55% realized win rate. A small backtest sample can look impressive by chance.

✓ Strict chronological processing
✓ No game outcome included in its own prediction
✓ Pushes handled separately
✓ Closing lines labeled as historical
△ Closing-line tests are not evidence of achievable earlier prices
△ Team-only baseline; no injury, QB or weather feeds yet
△ Same-season calibration means probabilities are provisional
Backtest selection assumes the historical closing line could have been wagered at -110, which is an evaluation benchmark, not a tradable-price guarantee.

Predictive architecture · baseline

Team strength: exponentially adjusted point-differential ratings.
Home advantage: fixed +1.5 points.
Update: after every game, each team's rating changes by 0.22 × margin surprise, split across opponents.
Offseason: ratings regress 35% toward league average.
Scores: smoothed recent points for/against create a total, constrained to 28–65 points.
Probability: normal approximation using rolling forecast residual dispersion, at least 11.5 points.
Signal: predicted margin versus the recorded market line.
Selection: pick only when modeled cover probability ≥ 55%.

Important omissions

The free odds collector checks public ESPN listings and the DraftKings Network popular-props page. It does not ingest QB availability, injuries, EPA/play or weather. It will not automatically update itself while the file is closed. This is the initial historical-data-based model.

To improve predictive quality, add a scheduled data pipeline and evaluate stronger models against this baseline out of sample.
